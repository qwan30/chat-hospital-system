# Wound Care Protocol

## Purpose
Guide structured wound assessment and dressing documentation for clinical workflow testing. Synthetic guideline only.

## Assessment
Document location, size, depth, drainage, odor, peri-wound skin, pain, temperature, and signs of infection. Photograph only if policy allows and consent requirements are satisfied.

## Dressing Change
Use clean or sterile technique according to wound type. Verify allergies such as latex or adhesive sensitivity. Label dressing with date, time, and initials.

## Escalation
Escalate for spreading erythema, fever, purulent drainage, necrosis, dehiscence, uncontrolled bleeding, or severe pain.

## Data Capture
Structure notes so OCR and RAG can extract wound stage, site, measurement, drainage, and follow-up plan.
