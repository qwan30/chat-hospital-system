# Daily Vital Signs Tracking Protocol

## Purpose
Define consistent vital sign monitoring for inpatient and observation workflows. Synthetic data for HOSP-AI-001 testing.

## Routine Frequency
Record BP, HR, RR, Temp, SpO2, pain score, mental status, and weight when applicable. Standard stable patients: every 4-8 hours according to unit policy.

## Escalation Thresholds
Notify responsible clinician for SBP < 90 or > 180, HR < 50 or > 130, RR > 28, SpO2 < 92%, Temp >= 38.3 C, new confusion, or uncontrolled pain.

## AI Assistant Use
Use AI to summarize trends only after permission checks pass. AI output must cite source documents and must not replace clinician assessment.

## Charting Requirements
Document device type, oxygen flow, patient position, symptoms, and interventions. For abnormal readings, recheck and include notification trail.
