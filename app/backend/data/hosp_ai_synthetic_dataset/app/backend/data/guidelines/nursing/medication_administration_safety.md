# Medication Administration Safety Guideline

## Five Rights
Right patient, right medication, right dose, right route, right time. Check MRN and allergies before administration.

## High-Risk Medications
Anticoagulants, insulin, opioids, sedatives, concentrated electrolytes, and chemotherapy require enhanced verification.

## Lab-Linked Safety
Review eGFR, Cr, K, AST, ALT, glucose, INR when relevant. Hold or escalate when values conflict with medication parameters.

## AI Assistant Boundary
AI may surface interaction warnings and lab trends but does not authorize administration. Final decisions remain with licensed staff.

## Documentation
Record dose, time, route, response, adverse effects, and reason for omission or delay.
