# Fall Risk and Daily Care Plan Guideline

## Purpose
Standardize daily nursing care plan elements for safety-focused AI retrieval. Synthetic guideline only.

## Fall Risk Screening
Assess gait, cognition, toileting needs, orthostatic symptoms, sedating medications, prior falls, and equipment needs.

## Interventions
Use bed low position, call bell within reach, non-slip footwear, clutter reduction, assisted ambulation, and patient education.

## Care Plan Update
Update mobility goal, pain control, nutrition, hydration, skin checks, and discharge barriers each shift.

## Escalation
Notify clinician for new weakness, syncope, head injury, anticoagulant use after fall, or change in mental status.
